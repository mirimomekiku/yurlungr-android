# Design

Yurlungr's UI follows **Bento × Daylight**: light-first white cards on a cool grey ground (Daylight), with
colour-blocked tiles, Space Grotesk headings and chunky corners (Bento). Dark mode keeps the same layout on
a near-black ground with the tiles at full strength. The mockups that led here live on the design canvas;
this file is the reference for building screens.

## Colour

Colours are fixed in `ui/Theme.kt` (Material You dynamic colour is off so the tiles stay on-brand).

| Role | Light | Dark | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground (`background`) | `#F2F3F5` | `#0C0C0F` | Screen background |
| Card (`surfaceContainer`) | `#FFFFFF` | `#18181D` | Cards, groups, header tiles |
| Tint (`surfaceContainerHigh`) | `#EEF0F4` | `#27272A` | Secondary fills, tracks |
| Ink (`primary`) | `#0F172A` | `#F4F4F5` | Primary buttons, user bubbles, selected chips |
| Line (`outlineVariant`) | `#E3E6EB` | `#27272A` | Hairline borders, dividers |
| Muted (`onSurfaceVariant`) | `#5B6474` | `#A1A1AA` | Secondary text |

**Tiles** (`AppColors.tiles`, each a `Tile(fill, on)`):

| Tile | Light | Dark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lime | `#D4F36B` | `#D4F36B` | Highlight, send, build, success |
| Lilac | `#DCD3FF` | `#C9B8FF` | Agents, branches |
| Peach | `#FFD3B5` | `#FFB98C` | Needs attention, permission prompts (`tertiaryContainer`) |
| Sky | `#C7E9FF` | `#8FD6FF` | Info |

**Status** (`AppColors.status`): success and warning colours (and their containers) for both themes, for
places where a tile would be too loud.

## Type

| Style | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display, headline, title (large/medium) | Space Grotesk | Bold/semibold, −0.5 sp tracking |
| Title small, body, labels | Inter | −0.25 sp tracking to stay legible at small sizes |
| Code, editor, terminal | Monospace | |

Both are bundled variable fonts in `res/font` (SIL OFL 1.1, licences in `assets/licenses/`).

## Shape

Cards 24 dp, stat and suggestion tiles 20–22 dp, controls and header tiles 16 dp, chips and status pills
fully round. Headers use 46 dp square tiles.

## Components

Shared building blocks in `ui/Common.kt` and `ui/Chips.kt`:

- `ScreenHeader` (☰/‹ tile + large title + actions), `HeaderTile`, `NavTile`
- `Group` / `GroupRow` (settings-style rows, optionally with an icon on a `tile`), `SectionLabel`
- `StatTile` (big number on a colour tile), `TilePill`, `StatusLabel`, `WarningBanner`
- `Chip`, `MenuChip` (pill pickers with a dropdown)

Screens compose these rather than styling from scratch.

## Motion and loading

`ui/Motion.kt` holds the timing tokens and screen transitions (direction-aware slides for push/pop,
crossfade between sidebar places). There are no spinners: `ui/Loaders.kt` has `WaveDots`, `FlowBar` and
`SmoothProgressBar`, and `ui/Skeleton.kt` has shimmer placeholders shaped like the content they replace.

## Icons

[Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com) icons are vendored as `ImageVector`s in `ui/PhosphorIcons.kt`,
generated by `tools/phosphor-gen.py` (see [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md)). Device glyphs that Phosphor
draws poorly in Compose are hand-drawn strokes in `ui/DeviceIcons.kt`. Agent logos are bundled PNGs from
LobeHub Icons (`agents/AgentBadge.kt`), shown without a container.

## Mascot

The mascot and launcher icon are the **Pixel Noir** rainbow serpent: 2.5D pixel art, red at the tail to
violet at the head, one narrow glowing eye, no mouth. The brand voice is mysterious, never cute (no smiles,
open mouths or tongues). The launcher icon is adaptive (`res/mipmap-anydpi-v26`) on a `#020104`
background, with square and round PNGs for Android 7.
